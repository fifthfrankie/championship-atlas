Display permission for football-data.org is owner-stated, not agent-verified. Discovery requests: 23 of 40 on docs and keyless sources, plus keyed probes: football-data.org 5 calls (1 failed with an unquoted-key bug of mine), API-Football 4 calls; M5 Sports Reference 1 of 6, a separate cap (see `discovery-requests.md`).
Current season resolved from the source: **2026-27** (Football-Data.co.uk file `2627/E1.csv` holds matches to 20/09/2026; Wikipedia page `2026-27 EFL Championship`; TheSportsDB season `2026-2027`).

Legend: Supported = verified data and a permitted route (where permission is owner-stated rather than agent-verified, the row says so). Partial = usable with documented gaps. Unavailable = no verified permitted $0 route. Blocked = needs an owner action.

## Product features

| Feature | Class | Route and gaps |
|---|---|---|
| Fixtures and results, current season | Supported | football-data.org. Display on the owner's verdict with attribution; raw snapshots not redistributed |
| Long league history, 2000-01 onwards | **Supported** (M7) | openfootball/england (CC0): final tables for 27 seasons computed from results by the same standings code, points deductions read from each season's final Wikipedia table, play-off ties, champions and every club's finishing position since 2000-01. Results only: no goals scorers, lineups or match pages for the seasons before the football-data.org window |
| Standings with adjustments | Supported (display on the owner's verdict) | Derived from results by `atlas/standings.py`, reconciled against `/standings` snapshot; deductions need a cited adjustment |
| Teams | Supported (display on the owner's verdict) | football-data.org |
| Match detail beyond score and half-time | Unavailable | No goals, cards or lineups in the free matches response |
| Player pages, current season | Partial | Squad bio, the provider's top 10 scorers, and (for matched players at 20 of 24 clubs) appearances and goals from Wikipedia tables. No minutes or match-by-match contribution |
| Player appearances and history | Partial, 2022 to 2024 only | API-Football, request-hungry, terms unverified |
| Player appearances and goals, current season | **Partial** | Wikipedia club season tables: 22 of 24 clubs, as-of dates 18 September to 10 October, community-maintained and verified against match results. About 85% of listed players are matched to squad players; unmatched rows still appear on the club page |
| Player career history | **Partial** (M7) | Each linked player's own Wikipedia article: senior, youth and international career tables with loan flags, plus date of birth, birthplace and height where given. 509 of 677 squad players. Linked by club and identical full name with agreeing nationality or position, or by club and date of birth; refused when the dates of birth disagree (14 cases), kept with a visible note when they differ by a typing slip (18 cases). Career appearances are as the editors wrote them, not minutes |
| Player minutes, starts, cards, per-90 (current season) | Dropped (owner decision) | FBref sits behind a Cloudflare managed challenge (browser-header retest still 403); the only automated route is a banned bypass. Alternatives: worldfootball.net has minutes but targets AI crawlers in robots (grey), statbunker is robots-permitted but has no minutes. Dropped by the owner on 2026-10-11, see STATUS.md |
| Club change report | Supported locally | Points, goals for and against from results (latest 5 vs previous 5) |
| Advanced metrics (xG, passing, shot maps) | Unavailable for current season | API-Football 2024 has shots and passes per player, but no current-season route and no xG model identified |
